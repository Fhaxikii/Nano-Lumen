## v1.97 (Beta)

### Fixed
- Fixed `WEIGHTS_FORMAT_REJECTED` on fresh environments: transformers 5.x refuses .bin weights (CVE-2025-32434), and mirrors may resolve to a .bin-only snapshot, breaking RAG retrieval with no self-heal on restart. Embedding and reranker models now explicitly fetch safetensors-only snapshots and load from local paths.

### 修复
- 修复新环境首次初始化知识库时报 WEIGHTS_FORMAT_REJECTED 的问题：transformers 5.x 出于安全策略（CVE-2025-32434）拒绝加载 .bin 权重，而镜像源解析出的快照可能只有 .bin 格式，导致 RAG 检索不可用且重启无法自愈。现在嵌入与重排模型改为显式获取 safetensors 快照后从本地加载，不再依赖上游的格式回退顺序
